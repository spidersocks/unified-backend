# Classes for 6–7 years (P1)

Suitable classes
- English Language Arts — grammar, comprehension, composition. Tuition: HK$4,500 / 8×45m; HK$7,500–$8,500 / 10×1h.
- Chinese Language Programs — reading comprehension, writing (stroke order, sentence structure). Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.
- Clevercal Math — arithmetic, geometry, problem solving. Tuition: HK$3,500–$4,500 / 10×45m; HK$4,500–$6,000 / 10×1h.
- Chinese Writing Programs — weekly dedicated writing by level.

Notes
- Small groups (4–6). Placement confirms best level/track.
