# Classes for 28 months – 3 years (late PN → K1 readiness)

Suitable classes
- Little Linguist — early English communication, alphabet. 45m.
- Little Sound Explorer — early phonics a–z. 45m.
- Chinese Character Recognition (寶寶愛認字) — early literacy (2–5 yrs; PN–K3). 45–60m. Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.

Notes
- Small groups (typically 4–6). Placement confirms fit/level.
