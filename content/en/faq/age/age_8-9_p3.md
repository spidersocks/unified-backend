# Classes for 8–9 years (P3)

Suitable classes
- English Language Arts — composition and comprehension depth. ~60m.
- Chinese Language Arts (中文語文課) — classical texts / advanced composition. Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.
- Chinese Writing Programs — richer narrative/expository; rhetorical devices.
- Clevercal Math — geometry, intro algebra, applications. ~60m.
- GAPSK Advanced (as relevant) — pronunciation, reading passages, full‑sentence responses.

Notes
- Small groups (4–6). Placement confirms level.
