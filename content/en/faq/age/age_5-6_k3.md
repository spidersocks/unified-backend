# Classes for 5–6 years (K3)

Suitable classes
- Phonics (upper levels) — split digraphs, sight words. ~60m.
- Chinese Language Programs — fuller sentences, reading expansion; pinyin consolidation for Mandarin learners. Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.
- Clevercal Math — concept + practice. 45–60m.
- Mandarin Pinyin — reinforcement (as needed).
- Alludio Educational Games — 50m.

Notes
- Small groups (4–6). Placement confirms level/timetable.
