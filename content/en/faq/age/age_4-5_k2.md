# Classes for 4–5 years (K2)

Suitable classes
- Phonics (progressive) — PGC, CCVC/CVCC, digraphs. 45–60m. Tuition: HK$5,200 / 8×45m; HK$7,500–$8,500 / 10×1h.
- Mandarin Pinyin (魔法拼音班) — K2+. Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.
- Chinese Language Programs — listening/speaking, recognition, writing basics. Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.
- Clevercal Math — conceptual foundations. 45–60m.
- Alludio Educational Games — game-based learning (3–6 yrs). Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×50m.

Notes
- Small groups (4–6). Placement confirms level.
