Use this quick reference to see which classes typically suit each age. Placement is confirmed by a short diagnostic assessment.

Note: Class sizes are small (usually 4–6). Typical lesson length: 45 minutes (PN–K1) and 60 minutes (K2+). Fees below follow the course pages.

## 18–28 months (PN, toddlers)
- Playgroups (幼兒班) — sensory/social development; 1 hour (30+30).  
  Tuition: HK$6,000 / 12 sessions.

## 28 months – 3 years (late PN → K1 readiness)
- Little Linguist — early English communication, alphabet; 45 minutes.  
- Little Sound Explorer — early phonics a–z; 45 minutes.  
- Chinese Character Recognition (2–5 yrs; PN–K3) — early literacy; 45–60 minutes.  
  Tuition (Chinese Character Recognition): HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.

## 3–4 years (K1)
- Phonics (Level 1–2 as suitable) — foundational sounds and blending; 45 minutes.  
  Tuition: HK$5,200 / 8×45m; HK$7,500–$8,500 / 10×1h.  
- Clevercal Math (early numeracy) — counting, quantities, shapes, measurement words.  
  Tuition: HK$3,500–$4,500 / 10×45m; HK$4,500–$6,000 / 10×1h.  
- Chinese Character Recognition (2–5 yrs; PN–K3) — recognition and reading readiness.

## 4–5 years (K2)
- Phonics (progressive levels) — PGC, CCVC/CVCC, digraphs; 45–60 minutes.  
  Tuition: HK$5,200 / 8×45m; HK$7,500–$8,500 / 10×1h.  
- Mandarin Pinyin (魔法拼音班) — intensive pinyin (K2+).  
  Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.  
- Chinese Language Programs — listening/speaking, character recognition, writing basics; Putonghua or Cantonese.  
  Tuition (typical Chinese group): HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.  
- Clevercal Math — conceptual math foundation; 45–60 minutes.  
- Alludio Educational Games — game-based learning (3–6 yrs).  
  Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×50m.

## 5–6 years (K3)
- Phonics (upper levels) — double/split digraphs, sight words; 60 minutes typical.  
- Chinese Language Programs — fuller sentences, reading expansion; pinyin consolidation for Mandarin learners.  
  Tuition (typical Chinese group): HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.  
- Clevercal Math — structured conceptual + practice; 45–60 minutes.  
- Mandarin Pinyin — reinforcement for tones/initials/finals (if needed).  
- Alludio Educational Games — 50 minutes.

## 6–7 years (P1)
- English Language Arts (英語語文課) — grammar, comprehension, composition.  
  Tuition: HK$4,500 / 8×45m; HK$7,500–$8,500 / 10×1h.  
- Chinese Language Programs — reading comprehension, writing (stroke order, sentence structure).  
  Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.  
- Clevercal Math — arithmetic, geometry, problem solving; 45–60 minutes.  
- Chinese Writing Programs — integrated writing in regular lessons; dedicated weekly writing classes available by level.

## 7–8 years (P2)
- English Language Arts — advanced grammar, reading, writing; 60 minutes.  
- Chinese Language Programs — 1500+ character progression, structured writing.  
- Chinese Writing Programs — narrative/expository; paragraph structure.  
- Clevercal Math — fractions, decimals, problem solving; 60 minutes.

## 8–9 years (P3)
- English Language Arts — composition and comprehension depth; 60 minutes.  
- Chinese Language Arts (中文語文課) — classical texts/advanced composition.  
  Tuition: HK$3,200 / 8×45m; HK$4,500 / 10×1h.  
- Chinese Writing Programs — richer narrative/expository; rhetorical devices.  
- Clevercal Math — geometry, intro algebra, application.  
- GAPSK Advanced (as relevant) — pronunciation, reading passages, full‑sentence responses.

## 9–10 years (P4)
- English Language Arts — critical thinking, extended writing.  
- Chinese Language Arts — advanced reading/writing and culture.  
- Chinese Writing Programs — expository/argument structure; cohesion.  
- Clevercal Math — continued progression; problem‑solving practice.

## 10–12 years (P5–P6)
- English Language Arts — advanced composition and comprehension.  
- Chinese Language Arts — advanced texts and writing; culture components.  
- Chinese Writing Programs — argumentative/application writing; advanced techniques.  
- Clevercal Math — upper primary consolidation; exam‑style practice.

## Special tracks (age‑dependent)
- Interview Preparation (P1 and school‑specific) — self‑intro, picture description, confidence.  
- Recitation Coaching (Cantonese/Putonghua) — diction, rhythm, stage presence.  
- AP Chinese (secondary students) — exam prep (reading/listening/speaking/writing).
